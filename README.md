# Customer Shopping Behaviour Dashboard

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of customer purchasing patterns using an interactive dashboard built with Power Query and Power Pivot. The goal is to extract meaningful insights from raw sales data and support data-driven decision-making.

**Project Overview**

The dashboard analyzes customer transactions, revenue distribution, and buying behavior across multiple dimensions such as:

1.Product Categories
2.Gender Contribution
3.Age Groups
4.Size Preferences
5.Payment Methods
6.Sub-category Performance

**📌 Key Insights**
1.Total Revenue: ₹2,33,081 generated from 3,900 transactions
2.Top Category: Clothing contributes the highest revenue
3.Customer Segment: Male customers contribute ~68% of total sales
4.Age Group: 19–37 is the most active and high-spending segment
5.Size Demand: Medium (M) size dominates with ~45% contribution
6.Payment Trends: Credit Card, PayPal, and Cash are equally preferred
7.Customer Retention: Avg. 25 previous purchases indicate strong repeat behavior
8.Improvement Area: Outerwear and Jewelry show lower performance

**🛠️ Tools & Technologies**
1.Power Query – Data cleaning & transformation
2.Power Pivot – Data modeling & DAX calculations
3.Excel Dashboarding – Visualization & reporting



**🎯 Business Value**

This dashboard helps in:

1.Identifying high-performing product categories
2.Understanding customer demographics
3.Optimizing inventory (size & category focus)
4.Improving marketing strategies
5.Enhancing customer satisfaction
